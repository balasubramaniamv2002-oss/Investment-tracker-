## 🌟 Overview

**Vestoria** is an all-in-one portfolio and net worth tracker built specifically for individual Indian investors. It consolidates investments across **NSE & BSE Equities, Index ETFs, Direct Mutual Funds, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB), MCX Physical Gold, Bank Fixed Deposits, PPF, Indian REITs, and Cash Reserves** into a single real-time valuation dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Native Indian Rupee (INR - ₹) Valuation & Formatting:**
   - Accurate Indian numbering system (`₹12,45,670.00`) with compact figures displayed in **Lakhs (L)** and **Crores (Cr)**.
2. **Universal Indian Ticker Engine (NSE / BSE):**
   - Pre-seeded directory of Nifty 50, Bank Nifty, Defense, Railways, IT, FMCG, and PSU leaders.
   - Dynamic ticker search and one-click **"Add Any Ticker to Catalog"** supporting any listed symbol (e.g., `RELIANCE`, `TCS`, `RVNL`, `PAYTM`, `HAL`, `COCHINSHIP`, `IRCTC`, `ZOMATO`, `TRENT`, etc.).
3. **8 Distinct Asset Classes:**
   - **NSE & BSE Stocks:** Real-time stock valuation with day gain tracking.
   - **Indian ETFs:** Nifty BeES, Bank BeES, Gold BeES, IT BeES, Junior BeES, Nasdaq 100 in INR (`MON100`), Liquid BeES.
   - **Direct Mutual Funds:** AMFI daily NAV schemes.
   - **Sovereign Gold Bonds & Gold:** RBI SGB series (2.5% p.a. + tax-free capital gains) and MCX 24K spot gold.
   - **Fixed Deposits & PPF:** SBI & HDFC Bank term deposits, and tax-free Public Provident Fund.
   - **Indian REITs & Real Estate:** Embassy, Nexus Select, Brookfield REITs, and private residential properties.
   - **Crypto in INR:** BTC, ETH, and Polygon (`POL` / ex-MATIC) in INR.
   - **Liquid Cash:** Bank savings and liquid sweep accounts.
4. **Weighted Average Cost & Capital Gains Accounting:**
   - Realized vs. unrealized gains breakdown.
   - Indian Union Budget 2024 LTCG tax estimator (12.5% beyond the statutory ₹1.25 Lakh exemption).
5. **Interactive Charts:**
   - Portfolio history area/line chart with 1M, 6M, 1Y, and ALL timeframes and scrubbing cursor.
   - Diversification donut chart switchable by Asset Type, Sector, or Currency.
6. **Transactions & CSV Engine:**
   - Complete ledger for Buy, Sell, Dividend, Deposit, and Withdrawal.
   - CSV Import Wizard with column preview, row validation, and error reporting.
   - One-click instant CSV export.
7. **Milestone Goals & Volatility Alerts:**
   - Target amount and target date tracking with calculated monthly required savings pace and celebratory confetti.
   - Price alert engine (Price above, Price below, 24h change %) with simulated scheduled Cloud Function price refresh.
8. **Printable / Downloadable PDF Audit Reports:**
   - Print-optimized report for tax compliance and audit review.
